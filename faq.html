Frequently asked questions.

Straight answers about how QII One works, what to expect, and how to get started. If your question is not listed, please get in touch.

Working with us

Services and scope.

What services does QII One provide?

We cover the full evidence lifecycle: quantitative and qualitative data analysis, dashboards and data visualization, GIS and mapping, survey design and processing, research and report writing, translation and editing, and technology support such as website and tool builds.

Most clients combine two or three of these into a single engagement.

Who do you typically work with?

NGOs and UN agencies, government institutions, research groups, universities, startups, and private businesses. We support both international organizations working in Somalia and local institutions building their own evidence base.

Can you handle a project end to end?

Yes. We can design the methodology, collect or clean the data, run the analysis, and produce the final deliverable. If you already have a team, we can take on a single stage instead, such as analysis only or visualization only.

Data and security

How we treat your information.

How do you protect the data we share?

Project files are handled on access-controlled systems and shared only with the team members assigned to your work. We do not publish, republish, or reuse client data without written permission, and we can sign a non-disclosure agreement before you send any files.

Will our data be used in your public portfolio?

Only with your explicit approval, and typically after a project has been made public by your organization. Anonymous, aggregated findings may be showcased if you agree to it in writing.

Do you store data after a project ends?

We retain working files only as long as needed for revisions and agreed record-keeping, then delete them on request. Tell us your retention requirement at kickoff and we will follow it.

Process and delivery

Timelines, language, and pricing.

How long does a typical project take?

A focused analysis or dashboard usually takes one to two weeks. Larger studies with fieldwork, multiple datasets, or extensive reporting run four to eight weeks. We confirm a realistic timeline before work begins and flag any risk to it early.

Which languages do you work in?

English, Somali, and Arabic. This covers data collection instruments, reports, dashboards, and stakeholder communication.

How is pricing determined?

Pricing depends on scope, complexity, volume of data, and turnaround. We provide a written quote before starting, based on either a fixed project fee or a day rate for open-ended work. There are no charges beyond the agreed quote.

What do you need from us to give an accurate quote?

A short description of the decision you are trying to support, the data you already have, the expected deliverable, and your deadline. Rough answers are fine, we can help clarify the rest.

Still have a question?

Send us the details of your project and we will reply with clear next steps.
